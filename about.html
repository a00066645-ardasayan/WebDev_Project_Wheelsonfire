About Wheels on Fire

Hello, I am John O'Reilly and I am the proud owner of Wheels on Fire! This business has tons of history that everyone should know about!

The first owner of this shop was my very own father, Martin O'Reilly. My father was very interested in cars and was enthuastic about his work which is what brought all the attention to the business. After 36 years of owing this business, selling and showcasing hundreds of thousands of cars, he retired in 2005.

Here comes the better part. After he retired, I took over at a bright age of 22 and continued his work. I wanted to be just like him ,carrying own his businesses to success. Over the last couple years, I was lucky enough to get my hands on sports cars and SUV style cars which is what I sell today. We still even have the older style cars which has been preserved from the business was first established. This company is for everyone, from every interests. We all have a soft spot for cars thats for sure. So why dont you come on over and take a look at what we have to offer!
